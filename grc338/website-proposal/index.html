Introduction

Brooke's Books

Brooke’s Books is a small, cozy, independent bookstore that offers a carefully curated selection of books, a welcoming reading space, and a small café menu for guests to enjoy while they read or browse. We have books of all genres and for all ages to enjoy. Our mission is to create a community centered environment where readers can discover new stories, share in community and relax with a cup of coffee and a good book.

Target audience

The target audience for Brooke’s Books website includes teens, college students, adults, and families who enjoy reading and relaxing in a cozy café environment. The website will mainly be used by book lovers looking to browse available titles, check café menu options, find store hours and location information.

Our target audiences goals are to have a cozy spot to hangout, study, read and drink good coffee.
